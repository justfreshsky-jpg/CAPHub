Why this exists

Why a CAP member would build free tools for CAP, and what's NOT in scope.

The thinking

  • CAP runs on volunteer evenings. Every hour spent looking up a CAPR section is an hour not spent flying, mentoring, or training. AI is finally good enough to give those hours back.
  • The publication references are publicly available. CAP NHQ publishes its current forms, regulations, manuals, and pamphlet indexes online, so members can verify every referenced document at the source.
  • NHQ doesn't build tools like these. NHQ runs eServices and the auth backbone — they don't ship single-purpose member tools. That's the gap volunteers can fill.
  • I want to contribute beyond meetings. I'm a member. While my squadron meets weekly, my engineering skills can serve more than just one squadron — these tools work for any CAP member, anywhere.

What's deliberately out of scope

  • Anything during a live mission. SAR tasking, ICS forms in flight, ramp checks during ops — life-safety and USAF integration risks. Hard line.
  • Cadet PII / parental consent docs. CPP-regulated. Zero data of that kind crosses our backend.
  • eServices integration. No NHQ API; reverse-engineering = no. Tools complement eServices, never replace or scrape.
  • Anything that competes with NHQ's own systems. If NHQ builds it, we don't.
  • No member rosters uploaded. The tools take questions and inputs you'd be typing anyway. We never ask for member lists.
